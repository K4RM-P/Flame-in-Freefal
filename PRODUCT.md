# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Engineers, safety reviewers, and experiment planners managing spacecraft and lunar-habitat fire scenarios. They need to inspect what has actually been simulated, understand evidence and uncertainty, and decide what input or test should come next.

## Product Purpose

EmberAtlas shows what NASA's ISS fire experiments prove about cabin materials, where that proof stops, and simulates what lies beyond, so the next test is the right one. Three surfaces (Simulator, Evidence, Methods) make scenario, provenance, extrapolation and next-test state legible without inventing measurements or hiding missing inputs.

## Brand Personality

Instrumented, candid, mission-ready. The product should feel calm and precise under scrutiny, with uncertainty and gaps treated as first-class engineering information.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS analytics dashboards, decorative risk scores, fake completeness, and alarmist red/green summaries that conceal theory-only or unsourced values. Do not use mock data to make empty states look populated.

## Design Principles

- Show only what the current project and simulation outputs support.
- Make provenance, uncertainty, and missing inputs visible at the point of decision.
- Keep the simulator a dense, stable desktop workspace.
- Prefer explicit engineering language over inferred confidence.
- Preserve a usable path through empty, partial, and AI-unavailable states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Do not rely on color alone for state; preserve keyboard focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion behavior, semantic controls, and explicit no-data labels.
