# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Engineers, safety reviewers, and experiment planners managing spacecraft and lunar-habitat fire scenarios. They need to inspect what has actually been simulated, understand evidence and uncertainty, and decide what input or test should come next.

## Product Purpose

EmberAtlas is an SI-first fire simulation and review workspace grounded in PSI/NASA evidence. The dashboard makes project, scenario, mitigation, provenance, and next-test state legible without inventing measurements or hiding missing inputs.

## Brand Personality

Instrumented, candid, mission-ready. The product should feel calm and precise under review, with uncertainty and gaps treated as first-class engineering information.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS analytics dashboards, decorative risk scores, fake completeness, and alarmist red/green summaries that conceal theory-only or unsourced values. Do not use mock project data to make empty states look populated.

## Design Principles

- Show only what the current project and simulation outputs support.
- Make provenance, uncertainty, and missing inputs visible at the point of decision.
- Keep high-value review work in a dense, stable desktop workspace.
- Prefer explicit engineering language over inferred confidence.
- Preserve a usable path through empty, partial, and AI-unavailable states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Do not rely on color alone for state; preserve keyboard focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion behavior, semantic controls, and explicit no-data labels.
